# Project vision

A visitor should be able to follow a highlight across the bodywork, stop to inspect a detail, and see a paint choice take effect without losing their view of the car. The camera introduces the vehicle. The visitor controls the pace and can move directly to configuration.

Use a dark studio, broad reflections and restrained typography to keep the silhouette readable. On a narrow screen, reserve space for both the vehicle and the active control. Preserve model information and configuration choices through a semantic HTML interface when 3D is unavailable.

Deliver the experience with free runtime tools. Clear asset permissions, accessible controls and measured GPU performance are release criteria. The [roadmap](roadmap.md) introduces one demonstrable outcome at a time.
